01

Mayfair · Candlelit French · ££££

The House of Wonder

Candlelit French fine dining, made for long romantic dinners. Make sure to try the seafood.

Walk in under the painted ceiling and the room does the rest. Tables are lit by small red lamps, the banquettes are deep, and nobody hurries you.

Order The shellfish to start, a whole fish to share, then the soufflé.

Read the review
02

Victoria · Innovative Chinese · ££££

House of Ming

Sichuan and Cantonese cooking in a jewel box of a room. Book the private dining room for a celebration.

Green silk walls, a crystal chandelier and a menu that moves between Sichuan heat and Cantonese finesse.

Order Dim sum to start, then the Sichuan dishes.

Three oysters
03

Mayfair · Classic seafood · ££££

Scott's

The grand seafood room on Mount Street. Sit at the oyster bar and order as much as you dare.

A Mayfair institution with a terrace that is always full and an oyster bar that is the best seat in the house.

Order Oysters at the bar, then the Dover sole.

Read the review
04

Mayfair · Champagne and martinis · £££

Bar Wonder

A low-lit bar for the first drink of the night. The martini is poured at the table.

Small, dark and quiet enough to talk. Arrive early for a seat at the bar.

Order The martini.
