    Dinner with friends

    Big tables, sharing plates and somewhere to go afterwards.

    By The Wonder List editors · Updated 24 November 2026 · 4 places

    For the nights when the group chat finally agrees on a date. Big tables, plates built for sharing and a bar nearby for afterwards.

    01

    St James's · Inventive British · £££

    Fallow

    Clever, generous cooking from an open kitchen. Go with friends and order widely.

    A lively room with counter seats facing the chefs. The menu changes often and rewards the curious.

    Order Corn ribs and the mushroom parfait.

    Read the review
    02

    Mayfair · Seafood galore · £££

    Saltie Girl

    Lobster rolls, towers of shellfish and a long list of tinned fish. Cheerful and unstuffy.

    A bright seafood bar where the lobster roll is the order and the tinned fish list is worth exploring.

    Order The lobster roll.

    Read the review
    A glass of champagne
    03

    Marylebone · Eastern Mediterranean · £££

    Delamina

    Bright, generous plates to share. Good for a long catch-up.

    A relaxed neighbourhood room serving Eastern Mediterranean dishes built for the middle of the table.

    Order A spread of small plates for the table.

    Read the review
    04

    Soho · Late-night cocktails · ££

    The Night List

    Where the evening goes after dinner. Loud, late and very good fun.

    A basement bar with a short cocktail list and a DJ from ten. Tables are held for members.

    Order A round of the house cocktails.
